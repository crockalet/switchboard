## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-23

- Every named .localhost service from portless on the shelf, with its port and whether it is up.
- Start, stop and restart from the shelf and the menu bar once the companion CLI is installed.
- A HUD that confirms each start, stop or restart, or says why it failed, and a log view for each service.
- Refreshes at your interval only while the list is on screen, and once a minute otherwise.
- Sends the companion's token with every request, and follows a changed port or token without a restart.
